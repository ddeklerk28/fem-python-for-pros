# Looping Through Collections

## Learning Outcomes

Iterate through lists, tuples, sets, and dictionaries, choosing a clear iteration pattern for the task.

## Learn - 30 minutes

- Watch the Looping Through Collections lesson.
- Compare iterating over dictionary keys, values, and key-value pairs.
- Investigate `enumerate()` and `range()` where relevant.
- Predict how collection ordering affects displayed output.

## Practice - 30 minutes

Given a collection of tasks, print a useful summary and find tasks matching a simple condition. Try iterating over a dictionary in more than one way.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The script runs from the terminal.
- You can explain what each loop iterates over.
- You can find a matching item without assuming every collection is indexed.
- You can describe any ordering assumptions in the output.

## Scope Notes

Prefer a direct loop when it is the clearest option. Comprehensions are introduced in the integration exercise.
