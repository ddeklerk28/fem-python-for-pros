# Tuples, Sets, and Dictionaries

## Learning Outcomes

Explain the purpose and behavior of tuples, sets, and dictionaries, and choose a suitable collection for a simple task-related problem.

## Learn - 30 minutes

- Watch the Tuples, Sets, and Dictionaries lesson.
- Compare these collections with lists.
- Investigate ordering, mutability, uniqueness, indexing, and key lookup.
- Consider why dictionary keys and set elements must be hashable.

## Practice - 30 minutes

Represent a small group of tasks using more than one collection type. Choose the structure based on how the data will be used, rather than using each type just to demonstrate its syntax.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The exercise runs from the terminal.
- You can explain why each chosen collection fits its purpose.
- You can predict what happens when values are repeated or a key is missing.
- You can distinguish accessing an item by position from looking it up by key.

## Scope Notes

Do not introduce loops or comprehensions as the main focus yet; those are covered in later exercises.
